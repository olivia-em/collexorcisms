parasite

You said our friendship meant the world to you.
And maybe, yes, it could be true. 
but still but still

before, another you
poisoned by some spiteful pill, 
admit my punishment 
for what I’d somehow done to you… 

a festered wound.

So you agree? Of course you do. 
You’ll bite on anything you can’t chew.

years and tears, wasted, down the drain
my always honest heartbreak 
disfiguring my face 
sitting on my tongue, my words, 
never could get through

You choke on things you’ll never feel.

so swallow them 
swallow truths
runaway, through alleyways
run from my love for you

care catches like a cold 
like a sickness, like the flu
purge and purge and then remember
there are conditions to it too

So you’d do “anything” to fix it?
when just later, you, betrayer
did you did, exactly 
what I’d asked you not to do?

So tell me, what is the truth? 
Did you ever love me too? 
I think you did, 
as much a jealous man could love a friend worth two.
