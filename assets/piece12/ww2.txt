we were two

You said our friendship meant the world to you.
And maybe, yes, it could be true. 
but still but still

before, another you
poisoned by some spite, admitted punishment 
for what (you think) I’d somehow done to you… 

a nasty festered wound.

So you agree? Of course you do. 
You’ll bite on anything you can’t chew.

years and tears, wasted, down the drain
my always honest heartbreak 
disfiguring my face 
always on my tongue, my words, 
never could get through
out the alley door, 
a runaway from pain, 
and from my love for you.
and from your second face.

You’d do “anything” to fix it.
just later, you, betrayer
did you did, exactly 
as I’d asked you not to do

So tell me, what’s the truth? 
Did you ever love me too? 
I think you did, 
as much a jealous man could love a friend worth two.
