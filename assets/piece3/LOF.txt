In the summer of 2025, around the same time I was writing My Familiar, I was also writing Lack of Flight. I wanted to submit a couple poems to some journal, and the theme was liminal spaces and hallucinations. 

This was before Collected Exorcisms became my thesis, and while that submission didn't work out, these poems became the basis of this collection, and set the tone in a lot of ways. 

I wrote Lack of Flight about a set of stairs in my father's house. I was trying to access a specific moment in time when I sat at the top, listening to what was going on downstairs. It's easy for me to go back there, to that memory, likely due to some unresolved emotions.
