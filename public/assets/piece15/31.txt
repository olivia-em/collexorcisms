31

You are to me
equal to a god.
Whoever I place opposite,
pales.

Ichor shouldn’t tarnish.

Our laughter, the tempo
of my heartbeat,
but I remember when
you stole my sound.

Now, my liquid tongue
has put it all to rest.
From your pedestal,
you’ve stepped down. 

My eyes, glitter gone.
My ears… Can you forgive me?
My body, spins & spins & spins &
I’ve hit the ground.

I am bluer than I’ve ever been,
than water, fast, below our bridge.
And when I say  “I love you” to the wind, 
it knows. 	

Just this once, I’ve let it go.
