I wrote CASS&RA in the spring of 2025, as an assignment. Originally it was a bonus poem for my other work, Lessons in Perspective (href="http://lessons-inperspective.netlify.app/"). 

This subject, a recurring character. 

Have you ever had someone break your heart?

Of course you have. 

What about your brain?

He met me, misunderstood me, left, and lied... to himself as well as to me. 

And he was mean while he did it. 

It felt malicious.
It made me miserable.

It made me question what I was worth at all.
