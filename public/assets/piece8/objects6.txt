all my deficiencies
a paper plane receipt, on it dust collecting 
enough to hold me in place

all that I’m lacking
a wood-cut print, torn and falling 
I can’t be rid, but I can make space.

these are my failings 
a necklace in my pocket, a kind of following 
I can’t let go, but I’m sickened by the chase.

afraid of you leaving
but you were never there, were you?
you is just a collection of things, 
a collection of waste.

if I stand for long enough, 
all statuesque, in contrapasso
up high, and so untouchable, 
right back up & on my pedestal

I will collect myself, I will. And suture up a pretty face.

what makes an object artifact 
time and time and time and
how could I possibly last forever
time and time and time again 

a cheap antique, just put on sale
there are no buyers, but some returners
they love to look, a quiet watching

my exotic wear and tear, all on a table pale
It’s far too pricey to dissect her
It’s enough for me, the window-shopping

So can I watch? Can I look? Can I play?
Film it for me, would you? This wretched thing, 
just for us, so strange, so lovely on display!
