all my deficiencies

a wood-cut print, from the wall falling 
I can’t be rid, but I can make space

these are my failings 
a necklace in my pocket, a kind of following 
I can’t let go, but I’m sickened by the chase

you were never really there, were you?
you is just a collection of things

if I stand for long enough

and suture up a pretty face
